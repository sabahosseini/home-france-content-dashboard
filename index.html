Is a €20K French House Really a Bargain?
EnglishResearch
Objective
Replace headline-price excitement with a complete affordability check.
Target audience
International buyers attracted by very low-priced rural French homes.
Hook
“A €20,000 house can become the most expensive bargain you ever buy.”
Must cover
  • Structural condition, roof, electricity, plumbing and diagnostics
  • Renovation and energy-upgrade budget
  • Acquisition costs, insurance, taxes and ongoing maintenance
  • Resale liquidity and year-round local demand
Audience questions
  • Is it liveable now?
  • How much renovation is hidden?
  • Why is it so cheap?
  • Could I resell it later?
Important nuance
Do not call a listing a bargain without a property-specific inspection, diagnostics review and total-cost estimate.
Production
60 sec · English · YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok · Owner: Unassigned
CTA
Ask Home France to evaluate the complete purchase—not only the listing price.

Never Buy a French Home From a Video Tour Alone
EnglishResearch
Objective
Show remote buyers how video viewings help—and what they cannot verify.
Target audience
Overseas buyers considering an offer after a Zoom or recorded viewing.
Hook
“A beautiful Zoom tour can show you the rooms. It cannot tell you everything you are buying.”
Must cover
  • Request a continuous room-by-room and exterior walkthrough
  • Inspect roof, boundaries, access, outbuildings, noise and surroundings
  • Review the diagnostic file and planning information
  • Use an independent in-person inspection before commitment
Audience questions
  • Can I safely buy remotely?
  • What can video hide?
  • Who should inspect for me?
  • When does an offer become binding?
Important nuance
Explain the actual purchase stage and legal effect of documents with a notaire; do not give a universal legal conclusion.
Production
60–75 sec · English · All short-form platforms · Owner: Unassigned
CTA
Home France can coordinate a buyer-focused viewing and local checks.

خانه ارزان در فرانسه؛ فرصت یا دردسر؟
فارسیتحقیق
هدف ویدیو
نشان‌دادن اینکه قیمت پایین بدون بررسی هزینه تعمیر و کیفیت زندگی معیار مناسبی برای خرید نیست.
مخاطب هدف
فارسی‌زبانانی که با دیدن خانه‌های بسیار ارزان روستایی به خرید ملک در فرانسه فکر می‌کنند.
هوک پیشنهادی
«خانه بیست‌هزار یورویی شاید ارزان باشد، اما آیا واقعاً می‌شود داخلش زندگی کرد؟»
نکات الزامی
  • وضعیت سقف، برق، لوله‌کشی و عایق
  • هزینه واقعی بازسازی و انرژی
  • فاصله از شهر، پزشک، فروشگاه و حمل‌ونقل
  • ارزش فروش دوباره و تقاضای محلی

  • چرا این خانه این‌قدر ارزان است؟
  • هزینه تعمیرش چقدر می‌شود؟
  • برای زندگی دائمی مناسب است؟
  • بعداً مشتری برای فروش دارد؟

The Best French Region Is Not a Map
EnglishHigh Signal
Objective
Turn region rankings into a personal decision framework.
Target audience
Foreign buyers and retirees deciding where in France to live.
Hook
“The best region in France is not the cheapest one—it is the one that still works for you on an ordinary Tuesday.”
Must cover
  • Doctor and hospital access
  • Airport, rail and everyday mobility
  • Shops, community, climate and year-round life
  • Employment needs versus retirement priorities
  • Trial stay before buying

  • How close should I be to an airport?
  • Which climate fits me?
  • Should I rent before I buy?
Important nuance
Regional maps are generalisations. Compare actual towns and services; avoid declaring one area best for everyone.

What a Complete French Property Tour Must Show
EnglishResearch
Objective
Create a buyer-first standard for remote property videos.
Target audience
International buyers using YouTube to shortlist French property.
Hook
“If the property tour skips the barn, roof, boundaries or defects, it is not a complete viewing.”
Must cover
  • Continuous walkthrough with every room and outbuilding
  • Roof, heating, windows, drainage and visible defects
  • Garden boundaries, neighbours, road and ambient noise
  • Exact language on septic or other compliance—not vague wording
  • Diagnostics and unresolved questions on screen
Audience questions
  • Why was part of the property skipped?
  • Does the septic system conform or not?
  • What defects are visible?
  • Can I request another live walkthrough?
Important nuance
A video is screening material, not a survey or legal verification.
Production

When Renovation Costs More Than the Home Is Worth
EnglishHigh Signal
Objective
Separate lifestyle value from financial return in renovation decisions.
Target audience
Foreign buyers considering barns, ruins and major renovation projects.
Hook
“Would you spend €700,000 renovating a home later valued at €500,000?”
Must cover
  • Purchase plus works plus contingency
  • Planning permission, trades, energy and timeline risks
  • Local resale ceiling and comparable properties
  • Lifestyle project versus investment project
Audience questions
  • How much contingency is enough?
  • Will renovations add equal resale value?
  • Can I manage works from abroad?
  • When should I walk away?
Important nuance
The €700K/€500K example is a viewer’s personal comment, not a verified valuation case study. Use it only as the discussion hook.
Production
60–75 sec · English · All short-form platforms · Owner: Unassigned
CTA
Build the acquisition and renovation budget before falling in love with the project.
